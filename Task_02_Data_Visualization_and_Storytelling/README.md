# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the Sample Superstore dataset using Power BI. An interactive dashboard was developed to explore sales performance, profitability, product categories, sub-categories, and regional trends.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful visual insights that support better business understanding and decision-making.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify sales trends over time.
- Compare performance across product categories and sub-categories.
- Evaluate regional sales and profit performance.
- Present key business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Dataset
- **Dataset:** Sample Superstore
- **Records:** 9,994 rows
- **Columns:** 21
- **Period:** 2014–2017

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV dataset

## Data Preparation
- Imported the dataset into Power BI.
- Converted date columns into the appropriate date format.
- Verified data types for numerical and categorical columns.
- Checked data quality, missing values, and errors.
- Created DAX measures for key performance indicators.

## Key Performance Indicators
The dashboard includes the following measures:
- **Total Sales:** Sum of sales.
- **Total Profit:** Sum of profit.
- **Total Orders:** Distinct count of order IDs.
- **Profit Margin:** Total Profit divided by Total Sales.

## Dashboard Visualizations
The interactive dashboard includes:
- KPI cards for overall business performance.
- Monthly sales trend from 2014 to 2017.
- Sales and profit comparison by product category.
- Profit analysis by sub-category.
- Regional sales and profit performance.
- Interactive slicers for Year, Region, and Category.

## Key Business Insights
- Technology is the leading category in sales and profit.
- The West region generates the highest sales and profit.
- Copiers are the most profitable sub-category.
- Tables are a major loss-making sub-category.
- The overall profit margin is approximately 12.47%.

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to analyze sales data and communicate business insights through interactive visualizations. The dashboard helps identify sales trends, profitable product segments, loss-making sub-categories, and regional performance.
